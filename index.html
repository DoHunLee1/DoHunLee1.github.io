About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at the Kim Jaechul Graduate School of AI, KAIST, advised by Prof. Jong Chul Ye. I received my M.S. in Artificial Intelligence from KAIST in Feb. 2026, and my B.S. in Computer Science from KAIST in Feb. 2024.

My research so far has focused on generative models, especially video diffusion models: improving temporal consistency without additional training, training video diffusion transformers with self-supervised vision encoders, and equipping video-to-video diffusion models with memory for consistent multi-turn editing (during my internship at Adobe Research). I have also worked on diffusion models for inverse problems, such as compressed sensing MRI.

These days, I am particularly interested in long-term world modeling, out-of-sight dynamics, and robotics.

Research Interests

  • Current: Long-term world modeling, out-of-sight dynamics, robotics
  • Previous: Video generation & editing with diffusion models, diffusion models for inverse problems
